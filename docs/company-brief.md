## Signature move: break the plan yourself

Two controls over the FY31 plan, both sourced from the whitepaper, both pure arithmetic on its rows:

- **Pull a row.** Remove any one bet. The total re-closes. Pulling the meter row lands on ₹520 Cr,
  which is the whitepaper's own figure for exactly that failure.
- **Flood the market.** Re-price every row at the whitepaper's crash values. The total falls to
  ~₹750 Cr and the defence row does not move.

Beside it, the whitepaper's own verdict on its own split, quoted: the total holds and the split does
not.

**Overlap, recorded rather than hidden.** A row that holds while others change is also the core of
`dg32-applications-catalog`'s plate ("external processors stays at 0"). The difference is the verb:
there, the reader filters a catalogue and a spec holds; here, the reader removes a bet or applies a
shock and a revenue line survives it. The held row is a consequence of the data, not the move.
