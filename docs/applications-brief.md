1. **Vibe.** Measured, exact, quietly confident. *(carried forward)*
2. **Journey.** The collection opens already in view. The reader filters it by domain, standard and

## Grammar: gallery / catalog

"Objects in a walkable collection. Museum labels, not marketing copy." The reader's question here is
genuinely *what are the options*, not *should I believe you*, which is the grammar's own test. Every
task gets the identical label schema, which is what makes it a collection rather than a grid.

Why the other seven lost:

- **Split stage** is the die page's grammar, shipped two commits ago.
- **Live surface** is retired, and its ban on a hero claim is what retired it.
- **Chaptered editorial** is `deepgrid-showcase`'s, and it wants chapters; a filter cuts across them.
- **Continuous world** requires worldflight video legs and forbids the acts this page needs.
- **Filmic one-shot** wants `scrub`, which needs footage of a part that does not exist yet.
- **Typographic poster** would throw away the four domain photographs.
- **Rhythmic cutlist** reads as energy; the energy answer is calm.

Its bans are honoured: no kinetic headline, no spotlight, no magnet, no scrub, and no persuasion in
a label. A label reads `Bearing race fault classification · ISO 13373 · 0.06 to 0.89 ms`, never
`Catch failures weeks early`.

## Signature move: the plate that never moves

A standing plate beside the collection recomputes as the filter changes: tasks in view, the latency
envelope they span, the standards they are tested against, and **external processors required: 0**.
Three numbers move. The fourth does not, and that is the page.

## Standing constraints, and one that bites here

Pre-silicon. Every figure carries its evidence kind, per `app/claims.ts`.

**The site claims 30 tasks and enumerates 16.** `useCaseDomains` declares 8 + 8 + 8 + 6 and carries
four examples per domain. The corpus supports the count itself: `deepgrid-dg32-ai-architecture.md`
states "24 of 30 use cases run >1 kHz" with a 10.24 ms worst case. No local source enumerates the
individual thirty.

So the catalogue holds the sixteen that exist, and says on its face that the corpus names thirty and
lists sixteen. Padding to thirty would mean inventing fourteen diagnostic tasks on a site whose
argument is that its numbers are checkable. The gap is stated where a reader can see it.
