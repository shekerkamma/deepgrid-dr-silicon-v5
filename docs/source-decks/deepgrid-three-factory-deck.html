GEOPOLITICAL SILICON DILEMMA
The $9B Mature-Node Import Trap Demands Multi-Factory Portability
Relying solely on foreign fabs risks export embargoes, while waiting for domestic fab modernization stalls commercial traction.
1. Foreign Fab Single-Sourcing Risk
  • • India imports $9B in mature-node chips (130nm–180nm) powering defense and aerospace.
  • • Western fabs are bound by ITAR and potential sudden export license revocations.
  • • Taiwanese fabs (TSMC/UMC) face severe geopolitical disruption risks across the Taiwan Strait.
  • • Indian military programs cannot build sovereign defense on fragile foreign silicon.
2. The Domestic Fab Reality (SCL)
  • • Semi-Conductor Laboratory (SCL) Mohali operates an operational 180nm CMOS/BCD line.
  • • However, prototyping every early digital experiment solely at SCL slows iteration cycles.
  • • Commercial fabless teams need fast, low-cost tapeouts to validate digital RTL quickly.
  • • The answer is a sequential bridge: prove digital IP globally, transfer sovereignty domestically.
3. The DeepGrid 3-Factory Thesis
  • • One standardized open-source design flow (OpenLane / Yosys / OpenROAD).
  • • Factory 1 (SkyWater): Fast, $14.3K MPW digital proof of silicon.
  • • Factory 2 (IHP Germany): World-class 350 GHz SiGe for 77 GHz radar.
  • • Factory 3 (SCL Mohali): Complete sovereign defense lock-in under DAP-2020 Make-II.

THE HONEST PORTING BOUNDARY
Digital Logic Moves By Script; Analog Circuits Require Physical Re-Engineering
DeepGrid rejects false marketing claims of 'instant zero-cost analog migration': physics dictates the real engineering boundary.
AUTOMATED: DIGITAL LOGIC
Portable Via Scripting (Automated)
  • • Verilog / SystemVerilog digital RTL codebases and synthesizable modules.
  • • DGridRiscV processor core architecture (RV32IM_Safety).
  • • AXI4 crossbar interconnect matrices, arbiters, and FIFO buffers.
  • • UVM verification testbenches, assertions, and coverage closure plans.
  • • Porting Mechanism: Automated Liberty (.lib) timing file swap + OpenROAD re-run.
  • • Engineering Overhead: <2 weeks of scripted timing closure per foundry retarget.
PHYSICS-BOUND: ANALOG & BCD
Requires Manual Re-Engineering (Physics-Bound)
  • • Bandgap Voltage References: Brokaw cells require resistor trimming for 12 ppm/°C.
  • • High-Voltage BCD Output Stages: 5V–120V LDMOS doping profiles differ per foundry.
  • • Foundry Non-Volatile Memory (NVM / OTP / EEPROM): Proprietary hard macros.
  • • ESD / EOS Pad Protection Rings: Must be redrawn and sized to meet ±15 kV HBM.
  • • Porting Mechanism: 1 to 2 dedicated calibration shuttle cycles per new foundry node.
  • • Engineering Overhead: 6–9 months of physical layout and silicon characterization.

OPEN-SOURCE EDA PORTABILITY
OpenLane, Yosys, And OpenROAD Slashes NRE And Enables Frictionless Retargeting
Automated physical design flow removes the multi-million-dollar tool barrier and locks IP into reproducible scripts.
1. Zero Tooling License Tax
  • • Proprietary EDA tool suites (Synopsys, Cadence) cost $1M–$2M per tapeout.
  • • DeepGrid's physical design operates 100% on open-source Yosys and OpenROAD.
  • • Eliminates per-seat licensing costs and legal export approval from US EDA vendors.
  • • Complete GDSII generation executes on local workstations in Hyderabad.
2. Automated Liberty (.lib) Swap
  • • When moving from SkyWater to SCL Mohali, the digital RTL remains completely untouched.
  • • Tool scripts swap target technology LEF files and standard-cell timing libraries (.lib).
  • • Floorplanning, clock-tree synthesis (CTS), and global routing execute deterministically.
  • • Guarantees timing closure at target clock boundaries (200 MHz fixed).
3. Fast Shuttle Compounding
  • • Multi-Project Wafer (MPW) shuttles allow rapid silicon validation at $14.3K per run.
  • • DeepGrid executes a complete design-to-silicon shuttle loop every 198 days.
  • • Validates 1,000 silicon-tested building blocks that compound into 50 approved SKUs.
  • • De-risks complex SoC subsystems before committing to volume SCL production.

DEFENCE PROCUREMENT LAW MOAT
Buy (Indian-IDDM) Grants Unconditional Monopoly Priority In Indian Defence Tenders
Foreign chipmakers (TI, STMicro, ADI) are legally excluded from top-tier sovereign military procurement.
1. Buy (Indian) vs Buy (IDDM)
  • • DAP-2020 establishes a rigid legal hierarchy for all Indian military procurement.
  • • SkyWater & IHP silicon qualifies as Buy (Indian) because design & IP are 100% Indian.
  • • SCL Mohali silicon unlocks Buy (Indian-IDDM): Indigenously Designed, Developed & Manufactured.
  • • Buy (Indian-IDDM) holds mandatory statutory priority over all other procurement tiers.
2. Positive Indigenisation Lists
  • • Ministry of Defence (MoD) has notified 5 Positive Indigenisation Lists banning imports.
  • • Covers drone flight controllers, high-reliability PMICs, and tactical communications ICs.
  • • Foreign suppliers cannot bid once a verified domestic IDDM alternative exists.
  • • Guarantees a legally protected domestic market for DeepGrid's mature-node SKUs.
3. SCL Mohali Sovereign Lock-in
  • • Domestic fabrication at SCL Mohali makes DeepGrid products completely sanction-proof.
  • • Zero ITAR dependencies; immune to US, European, or Chinese geopolitical embargoes.
  • • Secures long-term 15-to-20-year defense program availability.
  • • Establishes DeepGrid as the default semiconductor provider to BEL, DRDO, and MCEME.
