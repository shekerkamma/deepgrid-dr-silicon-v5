Subsystem Description

Samples all three DGCV32-RT cores on every clock cycle. When single-event upsets (SEU) from alpha particles or EMI power transients invert a bit in one core, the voter automatically passes the 2-out-of-3 consensus without halting execution.

Deterministic Safety Bound

Chassis brake-by-wire and steer-by-wire loops never stall. Discrepancies are logged in the Fault Collection & Control Unit (FCCU), and the faulty core is autonomously reset and resynchronized in the background.
